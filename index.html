Our Purpose

Why we exist

"Nature isn't a luxury. It's a right."

Eastside Gear Library was founded on a simple belief: the cost of a tent or a backpack shouldn't determine whether a family gets to spend a weekend under the stars.

Our specific purpose is to promote physical health, environmental stewardship, and equitable access to nature by operating a community lending library for outdoor recreational equipment — thereby removing financial barriers to the outdoors for residents of the Bellevue-Redmond area and surrounding communities.

The Region We Serve

Your backyard is extraordinary.

We're surrounded by mountains, lakes, trails, and rivers. The Cascades start practically at our doorstep. Eastside Gear Library exists to make sure everyone can experience it.

From Mount Rainer to Tiger Mountain, from North Cascades to Lake Sammamish — this region is one of the most outdoor-rich places in America. We're here to make it truly accessible for all.

What drives us

Three pillars,
one mission.

Everything we do flows from these commitments.

01

Physical Health

Time outdoors improves mental health, reduces stress, increases physical fitness, and builds community bonds. We believe every Eastside family deserves regular access to these benefits — not just those who can afford expensive gear.

02

Environmental Stewardship

Shared gear means less production, less waste, and a lighter footprint. By keeping equipment circulating through our community instead of sitting unused in garages, we collectively reduce our environmental impact.

03

Equitable Access

Quality outdoor gear costs hundreds — sometimes thousands — of dollars. That financial barrier shuts too many people out of the trails, rivers, and mountains surrounding us. We're tearing that barrier down.

The process

Simple as
borrow, explore, return.

No complicated sign-up. No gatekeeping. Just gear when you need it.

1

Become a member

Eastside residents register for a membership.

2

Choose your gear

Browse the collection and reserve what you need. Check availability and plan your adventure.

3

Pick it up

Grab your gear during library hours. Borrow for 7 days, with extensions available.

4

Return & share

Return it clean and ready. Every return makes the next person's adventure possible.
